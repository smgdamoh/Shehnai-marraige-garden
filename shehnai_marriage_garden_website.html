Welcome To Shehnai

Damoh's Most Trusted & Beautiful Marriage Venue

A Perfect Setting For Your Royal Celebrations

Situated at a convenient location near Saint Norbert School, Maganj Ward No 4, Damoh, Shehnai Marriage Garden is synonymous with elegance, hospitality, and memorable events.

Whether you are planning an extravagant Indian wedding, a warm engagement ceremony, a lively birthday bash, or a corporate gathering, our lush green open garden, decorated mandap area, and fully equipped AC guest rooms provide the perfect ambience.

Everything You Need For A Seamless Event

Lush Green Marriage Lawn

Expansive open-air garden capable of comfortably hosting 50 to 500+ guests with custom seating layouts, theme decor, and royal entrance gateways.

In-House & Custom Catering

Dedicated dining zone with clean kitchen facilities. Supports pure vegetarian traditional Indian thali, live food counters, chaat stalls, and desserts.

AC Guest Rooms & Bridal Suite

Furnished air-conditioned rooms with attached washrooms for the bride, groom, and close family members to relax and prepare in complete privacy.

100% Power Backup

Heavy-duty diesel generators ensure uninterrupted lighting, music, sound systems, and air conditioning throughout your function.

Wheelchair & Senior Accessible

Ramp access at entry points, wide pathways, and accessible seating to ensure comfortable movement for elderly guests and persons with disabilities.

Night Illumination & Sound

Stunning fairy lights, LED floodlights, focus stage illumination, and DJ-ready electrical setups to make night celebrations magical.
